About Us

History

Bo Rangers FC Sierra Leone was founded in 2020 with the aim of professionalizing local talent management. As the first licensed agency in the country, we have focused on building a bridge between aspiring local footballers and international opportunities. We are officially recognized by the SLFA.

Vision

To be the premier agency in West Africa, known for integrity, player development, and successful global placements for Sierra Leonean talent.

Mission

To nurture the athletic and professional careers of our players by providing expert negotiation, personal development support, and guaranteed exposure to top-tier clubs worldwide.

Our Team

Our management team consists of former professional players, certified agents, and youth development experts committed to the success of our athletes.
